## 1. A demo, not a shop

**BikeShop** is a **teaching demo** of the BFH Business School. The bike shop
is **fictional**:

- No bike is really rented, reserved or sold, and nothing is delivered.
- **No payments** are taken. Prices are shown and recorded only as part of the
  example.
- "API tokens" are play money for the demo: buying them costs nothing.

A booking or reservation in BikeShop creates **no contract** and no claim to a
bike.

## 2. Your account

- You are responsible for your password and for what is done with your account
  and your API keys. Keep keys secret and revoke those you no longer need.
- Use your own email address, or one ending in `.test` (never sent).
- Don't enter more personal data than the forms ask for, and nothing sensitive.

## 3. Acceptable use

Use BikeShop for learning and trying things out. Do not:

- try to access other people's accounts or data, or staff functions you were
  not given;
- overload the service or work around its rate limits;
- use it for anything unlawful.

Security findings are welcome: please report them to julius.kooistra@bfh.ch
rather than exploit them.

## 4. Availability and data

The demo is provided **as is**, without any guarantee of availability. It may
be changed, reset (including its database) or switched off at any time.
