# Renting a bike

Pick a bike on the **Rent** tab of the catalogue, choose when you pick it up
and bring it back, check the price, and press **Book**. You pay in the shop
when you collect the bike; the app only records the price.

## When you can pick up and return

Pick-up and return must both be **during opening hours**:

| day | hours |
| --- | --- |
| Monday to Friday | 09:00–18:30 |
| Saturday | 09:00–16:00 |
| Sunday and public holidays | closed |

The bike may stay with you overnight and over closed days; only the moments
you collect and return it must fall within opening hours.

More rules:

- Times are on a **15-minute grid**: 10:00, 10:15, 10:30, 10:45. The time
  lists only offer those.
- A rental lasts at least **1 hour** and at most **14 days**.
- You can book up to **60 days** ahead.
- You can hold at most **3** open rentals (booked or picked up) at once.

To see which bikes are free for a period, use **Check availability** on the
Rent tab: it lists the free bikes with their price for exactly that period.

## How the price is calculated

Every bike category has an **hourly rate** and a **daily rate**. The daily
rate is a cap:

1. Every **started hour** counts as a full hour (1 h 10 min is 2 hours).
2. Every full **24 hours** costs the daily rate.
3. The hours left over cost the hourly rate each, but **never more than the
   daily rate**.

Example for a city bike at CHF 8 per hour and CHF 35 per day:

| rental | calculation | price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 hour | 1 × 8 | CHF 8.00 |
| 3 hours | 3 × 8 | CHF 24.00 |
| 5 hours | 5 × 8 = 40, capped at the daily rate | CHF 35.00 |
| 26 hours | 1 day (35) + 2 × 8 | CHF 51.00 |
| 3 days | 3 × 35 | CHF 105.00 |

The bike page shows this breakdown as a **quote** while you choose the
times. Your booking keeps the price you were quoted, even if the shop
changes its price list later.

The **deposit** shown on the quote is taken in the shop when you collect the
bike and given back when you return it.

## Returning early or late

The shop works out the final price when you bring the bike back:

- **Early**: you pay the booked period anyway; there is no refund.
- **Late**: the extra time is charged at the same rates as your booking.

## Cancelling

Under **My rentals and orders**, press **Cancel** on a booking. Cancelling is
free as long as the rental hasn't started.
