# Buying a bike

Bikes for sale are on the **Buy** tab of the catalogue. You can filter by
category, frame size, condition (new or used), price and text.

## Reserving

The shop doesn't take payment online. Instead you **reserve** a bike:

1. Open the bike and press **Reserve**.
2. The shop holds the bike for you for **7 days**. The page shows the date
   it is held until, and you get a confirmation email.
3. Come to the shop within those 7 days, pay at the counter, and take the
   bike home.

While a bike is reserved, nobody else can reserve it. You can hold at most
**2** reservations at once.

## After 7 days

If you haven't collected the bike by then, the reservation **expires**: the
bike is for sale again and you get an email saying so. Nothing is charged.

## Cancelling

Changed your mind? Under **My rentals and orders**, press **Cancel** on the
reservation. The bike goes back on sale at once.

See also: [renting a bike](renting.md).
