# Getting started and signing in

## Create an account

1. Open the shop and choose **Create an account** on the sign-in page.
2. Pick a username, enter your email address and a password of at least
   **10 characters**.
3. Sign in with your new username and password.

The shop sends booking and reservation confirmations to your email address.
Use an address you read, or one ending in `.test` if you only want to try
things out: those are never sent.

## Signing in

Signing in gives this browser tab its own **API key**, a long secret code
that the page sends with every request. It is kept in the tab only:

- It is forgotten when you close the tab.
- It expires after one day; then the shop asks you to sign in again.
- Every tab signs in on its own, so signing out in one tab does not sign
  you out in another.

**Log out** (top right) cancels this tab's key at once. On a shared
computer, always log out.

## Finding your way

| menu | what you find there |
| --- | --- |
| Catalogue | bikes for sale (**Buy**) and for rent (**Rent**), with filters |
| My rentals and orders | your bookings and reservations, with **Cancel** |
| Account | your API tokens, usage and API keys |
